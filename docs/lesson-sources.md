# Sources for new lessons

Each line quotes an added lesson, followed by its NASA source.

## won

- Checking air, water, and power before trouble starts gives the crew more time to respond. — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/
- Saving power before the long lunar night helps equipment keep working when sunlight is gone. — https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/lrolitho7temperaturevariation27may2014.pdf
- Recycling water stretches the supplies a crew can carry from Earth. — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/
- A protected shelter gives astronauts a safer place to wait during a solar radiation storm. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/
- Keeping spare supplies and repair plans ready helps a distant crew handle surprises together. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/

## lost_oxygen

- Astronauts breathe oxygen and breathe out carbon dioxide, so cabin air needs both oxygen supply and carbon dioxide removal. — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/
- A working oxygen generator can split water to make breathable oxygen. — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/
- Air filters and carbon dioxide removal equipment need maintenance to keep cabin air safe. — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/
- A leak can lower cabin pressure, so checking seals is part of protecting the air supply. — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/
- Backup air supplies give the crew time to repair life support equipment. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/

## lost_power

- On much of the Moon, night lasts about 14 Earth days, so solar panels need stored power for the dark. — https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/lrolitho7temperaturevariation27may2014.pdf
- Dust can reduce the sunlight reaching solar panels on Mars. — https://www.nasa.gov/missions/martian-dust-could-help-explain-water-loss-plus-other-learnings-from-global-storm/
- Life support equipment needs electricity to make oxygen and clean water. — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/
- Grow lights need power to help plants produce food indoors. — https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/
- Keeping a power reserve helps the crew handle equipment problems before batteries run out. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/

## lost_food

- Plants need light, water, air, and nutrients to grow food in a space garden. — https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/
- NASA grows some fresh vegetables on the space station, but crews still need stored food. — https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/
- Careful watering keeps plant roots from drying out or drowning. — https://www.nasa.gov/exploration-research-and-technology/growing-plants-in-space/
- Recycled water can support drinking and food preparation while reducing waste. — https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/
- Keeping extra food makes it easier to handle a missed harvest or delayed resupply. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/

## lost_shielding

- Space radiation comes from the Sun and from beyond our solar system, so crews need places with stronger protection. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/
- Water can be placed around a living area to help block some space radiation. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/
- A shelter with extra shielding can protect astronauts during a solar particle storm. — https://science.nasa.gov/sun/solar-storms-and-flares/
- The Moon has little atmospheric protection, so habitat walls must help shield the crew. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/
- Radiation protection needs to be planned before astronauts travel far from Earth. — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/deep-space-habitation-overview/
