# Delta-V 26: Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

Delta-V 26 is a browser game where kids run a small space base. Players pick the Moon (Shackleton Crater) or Mars (Jezero Crater) and lead three astronauts, Maya, Leo and Zuri, through 30 sols. Each sol the crew has two jobs. They can make oxygen, fix the shield, grow food, rest or do science. Every job uses one resource to fill another, so players must plan ahead. Short bonus mini-games reward good work, and science points buy upgrades.

The Sun drives the story. The game uses real solar flare records from NASA's DONKI database. Players can live through the last 30 days of real flares (15 October to 13 November 2026) or the huge storms of May 2024. Each sol uses one real day: bigger flares mean stormier weather, and a forecast shows what is coming so players can prepare.

Events, facts and end-screen lessons explain real space ideas such as radiation, dust and shielding. The game is made for ages 10 to 16 and runs in a web browser with no install. It plays best on a laptop or a phone held sideways. Built for NASA Space Apps 2026 by team Delta V. This is an independent project and NASA does not endorse it.
